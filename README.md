## Featured Project

### AI Privacy Gateway

**AI-Privacy-Gateway** is a local-first privacy gateway designed to enable safer use of cloud AI services with sensitive data.

Rather than relying solely on an external AI provider to protect sensitive information, the gateway performs deterministic classification and sanitization locally before data is transmitted. Sensitive information can be blocked, internal identifiers tokenized, and approved public information preserved while sensitive mappings remain under local control.

Key design principles include:

* Fail-closed handling of sensitive information
* Deterministic data classification and sanitization
* Reversible tokenization of internal identifiers
* Local protection of sensitive mappings
* Structured-data protection for formats such as JSON, XML, and CSV
* Auditable processing
* Local AI integration
* Controlled use of cloud-based AI services
* Privacy by design

**Status:** Preparing for public release.
